# Speed experiments (autonomous run)

Goal: bring the overall time down without going below Q4. Dropping layers
is allowed. Every idea is listed with what was tried and what happened,
kept or not. Starting point and context: [performance.md](performance.md).

## Method

- **Device:** Cardputer-Adv, SD at 80 MHz (1-bit), model `lfm-q4-head-lr256.lfm`
  (Q4 everywhere, low-rank LM head shortlist), int8 activations, PIE SIMD.
- **Harness:** `tools/bench.py` uploads the prompt and `/cfg.txt` knobs, then
  reads the firmware's `RESULT` line. All runs are logged in `out/bench_log.jsonl`.
- **Benchmarks:**
  - **B1:** "The capital of France is" (6 tokens) until end-of-text. The
    answer is " Paris." + `<|im_end|>`, so 3 generated tokens.
  - **B2:** "Here is a list of the eight planets of the solar system, in order
    from the Sun:" (20 tokens), then 12 generated tokens (EOS ignored). The
    answer is "\n\n1. Mercury\n2. Venus…".
- **Quality checks** for lossy ideas run on the Mac with `lfm_eval`
  (perplexity, top-1 agreement over 419 tokens of mixed text).

## Baseline (start of the run)

| | B1 total | B1 prompt | per token (B2) | B2 prompt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| speculative, up to 5 drafts per pass | 41.27 s | 13.20 s | 14.06 s | 46.46 s |

## Results

| # | Idea | Result | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0a | Low-rank LM head shortlist (rank 256, top 256 re-scored) | LM head 37.7 → 17.8 MB per token; 99.5% agreement with the full head (host) | yes |
| 0b | Conv state on the SD card → prompt batch 6 fits | B1 prompt 25.7 → 13.2 s (one pass instead of two) | yes |
| 1 | Speculative decoding with WikiText n-gram table | B2: **0 of 85 drafts accepted**. The table is full of WikiText artifacts (`@,@`, spaces before commas) that the model never writes; the earlier 1.51 tokens/pass simulation was flattered by WikiText prompts | replaced by 5 |
| 2 | Speculative decoding off (plain greedy) | B1 38.6 s (12.7 s/token); B2 12.75 s/token vs 13.95 with useless drafts: verifying 6 positions per pass costs 1.2 s | new baseline |
| 3 | LCD progress bar off | B1 38.56 vs 38.60 s: no effect | kept on |
| 4 | Read block size (per buffer, x2) | B1 total: 16 KB 40.4 s, 24 KB 39.1 s, **32 KB 38.6 s**, 40 KB 48.5 s, 48 KB 48.3 s. 40/48 KB are 0.2 s/token faster, but RAM drops the prompt batch to 5 (2 passes) | 32 KB |
| 5 | **n-gram table from the model's own greedy text** (254k tokens generated by `tools/selfgen_corpus.py` from clean prompts; 96k trigrams, 2.2 MB in flash) | Simulation on 20 clean held-out prompts: 1.25 tokens/pass at k=2 (WikiText table: 1.15). Device B2: k=1 9.52, **k=2 8.54**, k=3 8.75 s/token (plain 12.75) | **yes, k=2** |
